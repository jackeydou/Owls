## Features

- Router: select text in a thread message and choose Quote to add a note. Quotes wait as tags above the editor and go out with your next message.
- Projects sidebar: an opened project lists its 5 latest workers, finished ones included. A row at the bottom loads 10 earlier workers at a time.

## Bug Fixes

- Window drag: the worker conversation in the right sidebar scrolls again. Only title bars drag the window now, not whole sidebars.
