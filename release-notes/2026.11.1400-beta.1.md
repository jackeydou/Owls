## Features

- The app is now named Owlto. It installs as a new app next to any earlier Owls install and does not update it in place. Owlto starts with empty data in `~/.owlto` and `~/Owlto`, asks you to sign in again, and requests macOS permissions again. Your earlier data stays untouched in `~/.owls` and `~/Owls`.
- The three-owl Trio logo is now the default app icon.

## Bug Fixes

- The close button of an open card no longer sits under the right sidebar toggle, and you can drag the card panel's left edge to make it wider or narrower.
