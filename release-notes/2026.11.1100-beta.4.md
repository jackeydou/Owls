## Features

- The worker panel is one conversation that leads with the worker's report. Tool calls collapse into one muted line you can expand, and a +/− change summary opens a diff for each file.
- The app icon and the Router, Claude Code, and Codex avatars are now hand-drawn doodle owls. Settings offers nine app logos. If you picked a logo before, it resets to the new default.

## Improvements

- Worker links in Router replies are tags tinted by the worker's status: blue when done, green while running, amber when waiting, red when failed, and gray when queued.

## Bug Fixes

- The Forward to a thread and Copy buttons on a message no longer overlap. They sit side by side in one hover toolbar.
