## Features

- A new Usage stats page in Settings shows the tokens each agent used per day, the cache hit rate, and the estimated cost. You can break the numbers down by agent, model, project, and role.

## Improvements

- The right sidebar switches between Projects and Cards from icons at its top, and the sidebar toggles match the color of the other icons.
- Hovering the Usage entry in the sidebar shows each agent's tightest limit as a small bar in the agent's color. Clicking opens the full panel, and clicking the sidebar closes it.

## Bug Fixes

- The buttons in the update popup respond to clicks again.
