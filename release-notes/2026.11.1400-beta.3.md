## Features

- You can archive a thread from the sidebar. Settings > Archives lists archived threads so you can open, unarchive, or delete them for good.
- Cards are now called Notes throughout the app.
- Settings has a new Experiments page. Its first switch adds a thread bar button that sorts the thread into memory right away, and a sleeping owl shows while that Dreaming runs.
- On/off settings now use a switch instead of a dropdown.
- The Router chat title bar lines up with the worker panel, and the worker conversation follows new messages, with a jump-to-latest button when you scroll up.
