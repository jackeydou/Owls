## Features

- Workers: a worker's conversation opens in the right sidebar, with its actions in a second bar. Clicking a project there folds or unfolds its workers.
- Updates: the update notice links to the new version's release notes. Check for Updates in the app menu now shows a small corner notice that follows the check, download, and ready steps instead of opening Settings.

## Bug Fixes

- Settings: the Settings sidebar no longer scrolls away with a long page.
