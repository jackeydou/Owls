## Features

- The worker panel shows when a worker is working. Messages you send while it is busy wait in a queue: steer one in right away, or let it go when the turn ends.
- Claude Code workers can use your projects and the Workspace without asking for folder access first. With Full access on, they skip every approval.
- Model lists in a project's settings are easier to manage: your picked models sit on top as a numbered list you can drag into order, and the rest are grouped by model family below, with search when the list is long.
