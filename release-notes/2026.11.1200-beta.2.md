## Features

- Knowledge cards: save a Router reply or a worker result as a card, organize cards on boards, and attach them to messages for the Router or a worker.
- A new Pull Requests list shows every pull request agents opened across your projects, with merge state, review, and CI status. Filter by merge state, checks, or project, and sort by what needs attention.

## Improvements

- The macOS menus follow the usual Mac layout: Settings and Check for Updates are in the Owls menu, new thread and project commands in File, the sidebar, Overview, and Pull Requests in View, and documentation and feedback links in Help. The menus follow your English or Chinese language setting.
- Corners are slightly less rounded across the app, including cards, menus, dialogs, inputs, buttons, and the composer.
