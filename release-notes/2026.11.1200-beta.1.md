## Improvements

- The title bar is simpler: the Overview status pill and the Router details button are gone.
- Both sidebars open wider by default and keep the width you drag them to.
- Overview cards have a white surface with project icons and owl avatars for the Router and workers.
- Workers in the Projects sidebar show their owl avatar, and running workers and Routers show an animated owl typing on a laptop.
- App icons use a thinner stroke and are slightly larger, and the sidebar toggles use plain sidebar icons.
