## Before You Install

- The Simulator panel needs Xcode with an iOS simulator installed on this Mac. Without Xcode, the panel says so and the rest of the app works as before.

## Features

- iOS Simulator: a Simulator panel in the right sidebar shows a booted iOS simulator live. Workers get simulator tools to install, launch, look at, and drive their app, with your OK per device and for installs below full access.
- iOS Simulator: works with Xcode 27. When Xcode disconnects the simulator's input, the panel says so and offers Repair input, and it tells you if the picture has not arrived after a few seconds.
