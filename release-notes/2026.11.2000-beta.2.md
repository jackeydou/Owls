## Before You Install

- Remote Router is new in this beta. Pairing over the Owlto relay needs a GitHub sign-in; pairing on the same network does not. The iOS app is not on the App Store yet.

## Features

- Remote Router: turn on Connect devices in Settings → Devices to pair another Mac or the iOS app, over the same network or the Owlto relay, and drive this Mac's Router from there.
- iOS app for Remote Router: read threads, message the Router, steer or stop workers, and answer requests from the Needs You inbox. It has a Doodle style in light and dark, and shows threads beside the list on iPad and an open iPhone Duo.
- Projects panel: hover a failed or blocked worker and click the check mark to mark it resolved; it comes back if it runs again and gets stuck.
- Router: while the Router works, its owl types and bubble dots bounce like a worker's, with a Stop button next to them.
