## Features

- Fewer approval cards. With Workspace access set to "Approve for me", Claude Code and Codex approve safe actions on their own and only ask you about the rest. Cursor offers Read only and Full access; if you had picked Workspace access for Cursor, it is now Full access.
- Owlto shows a notification and a dock badge when a worker is waiting for your approval or answer while the app is in the background. Click it to open that worker. You can turn this off in Router & Workers settings.
- The permission card is smaller, and under the Doodle style it looks like the website's "Needs your OK" strip.
